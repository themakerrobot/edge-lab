Our class story — sample material

[Our class rules]
Walk in the hallway, don't run. At lunch, take only as much as you can eat and don't leave food.
Open the windows during breaks to let fresh air in. Tidy your desk when class is over.
When a friend is talking, listen to the end before you speak.

[Our class timetable]
Morning activity starts at 8:40. First period is at 9:00, and lunch is at 12:20.
On Wednesdays, club activities start after fifth period. Sixth period on Friday is P.E.

[Why rainbows appear]
A rainbow forms when sunlight bends and reflects inside raindrops.
When the sun comes out after rain, stand with your back to the sun and you can see a half circle in the sky.
It shows seven colors: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo and violet.
Each color of light bends a little differently, so the colors spread apart.

[About the Moon]
The Moon is a satellite that goes around the Earth. One trip around the Earth takes about 27 days.
The Moon seems to change shape: crescent, half moon, full moon and waning moon.
It changes because the side that gets sunlight is different each day.
There is almost no air on the Moon, so sound cannot travel there.
On the Moon you would weigh about one sixth of what you weigh on Earth.

[What plants need to grow]
Plants need sunlight, water, air and the right temperature to grow.
Leaves use sunlight to make their own food. This is called photosynthesis.
Roots take in water and nutrients from the soil, and the stem carries them up to the leaves.
Too much water can make the roots unable to breathe, and the plant may wilt.

[How to recycle]
Take the tape off paper, flatten it and collect it. Take the cap and label off plastic bottles and crush them.
Paper cups with food on them and oily paper cannot be recycled.
Batteries and fluorescent lamps must be collected separately in the special bins.
